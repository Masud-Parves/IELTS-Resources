## [Day 4] -ARY vs -ERY and Other Confusing Endings

### Learning objectives

By the end of today you will be able to:

- spell **40 common -ary / -ery words**, plus the **-ory** and **-ry** words that sound the same;
- tell **stationary** from **stationery**, and **diary** from **dairy**;
- use **9 mini-lessons** on other endings that cost marks in IELTS: **-tion/-sion · -al/-el/-le · -ic/-ical · -cial/-tial · -ous/-ious · -ful/-fully · -or/-our/-er/-ar · -er/-re · -ise/-ize**.

> 💡 **How to study today:** Part A is the main lesson. Treat Part B as 9 short lessons of about 5 minutes each. Read the rule, say the examples aloud, and then do the matching exercise.

### Part A: -ARY vs -ERY (vs -ORY and -RY)

#### Why your ear can't help you

All four endings are reduced to /əri/ or just /ri/. In British English, the vowel often **disappears completely**:

| Word | IPA | Syllables you hear | Syllables you write |
|---|---|---|---|
| secretary | /ˈsekrətri/ | 3 | sec-re-ta-ry (4) |
| dictionary | /ˈdɪkʃənri/ | 3 | dic-tion-a-ry (4) |
| laboratory | /ləˈbɒrətri/ | 4 | lab-or-a-to-ry (5) |
| stationery | /ˈsteɪʃənri/ | 3 | sta-tion-e-ry (4) |
| library | /ˈlaɪbrəri/ or /ˈlaɪbri/ | 2–3 | li-bra-ry (3) |

#### The 4 rules

**Rule 1: -ary is the most common ending.** Most adjectives (*necessary, ordinary, temporary, primary, voluntary*) and many nouns (*library, dictionary, salary, summary*) end in **-ary**. If you have no other clue, choose **-ary**.

**Rule 2: -ery = a whole word + -(e)ry.** If you can see a complete word you know, it's usually **-ery**.

| Base word | -ERY word | Base word | -ERY word |
|---|---|---|---|
| bake | bak**ery** | deliver | deliver**y** |
| brew | brew**ery** | discover | discover**y** |
| machine | machin**ery** | recover | recover**y** |
| scene | scen**ery** | cook | cook**ery** |
| green | green**ery** | nurse | nurs**ery** |
| brave | brav**ery** | pot | pott**ery** |
| rob | robb**ery** | jewel | jewell**ery** (BrE) |

⚠️ **Learn these as a group:** gall**ery**, batt**ery**, myst**ery**, surg**ery**, cemet**ery** (3 e's), lott**ery**, celery. They have no base word.

**Rule 3: -ory usually has a related word with -or, -ion or -ive.**
direct**or** → direct**ory** · access → access**ory** · satisfact**ion** → satisfact**ory** · compuls**ion** → compuls**ory** · observat**ion** → observat**ory** · invent**ion** → invent**ory**.
Also learn: fact**ory**, hist**ory**, laborat**ory**, dormit**ory**, categ**ory**, territ**ory**, mem**ory**, the**ory**.

**Rule 4: -ry with no vowel.** If the base word ends in *-er* or *-t*, the vowel often disappears: ent**er** → **entry** ★, chemist → **chemistry**, poet → **poetry**, regist**er** → **registry**, minist**er** → **ministry**. Also **country**, **industry**, **pastry**.

#### The two IELTS traps

| Word | Meaning | Memory trick |
|---|---|---|
| **stationery** /ˈsteɪʃənri/ *n* | paper, pens, envelopes | **e** for **e**nvelopes |
| **stationary** /ˈsteɪʃənri/ *adj* | not moving | **a** for **a**t rest; a c**ar** p**ar**ked |
| **diary** /ˈdaɪəri/ *n* | a book for daily notes | **I** write in my d**i**ary (i before a) |
| **dairy** /ˈdeəri/ *n* | milk products; a milk farm | **ai**r: a cow breathes fresh **ai**r in a d**ai**ry |

### Part B: Nine more confusing endings

#### B1. -tion vs -sion vs -ssion vs -cian

All of these are pronounced /ʃən/ or /ʒən/, and the /ʒən/ sound is the only one that tells you the letter.

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| You hear **/ʒən/** (as in *vision*) → always **-sion** | deci**sion**, televi**sion**, revi**sion**, conclu**sion** ★, confu**sion**, occa**sion**, divi**sion**, ero**sion**, inva**sion** |
| Verb ends in **-d / -de / -se** → **-sion** | deci**de** → decision · conclu**de** → conclusion · exten**d** → extension · expan**d** → expansion · inclu**de** → inclusion · revi**se** → revision |
| Verb ends in **-mit** → **-mission** | ad**mit** → admission · per**mit** → permission · sub**mit** → submission · trans**mit** → transmission |
| Verb ends in **-ss** → **-ssion** | discu**ss** → discussion · expre**ss** → expression · impre**ss** → impression · profe**ss** → profession · posse**ss** → possession |
| **Everything else** → **-tion** (most common) | educa**te** → education · inform → information · regist**er** → registration · reser**ve** → reservation · pollu**te** → pollution · introdu**ce** → introduction · prescri**be** → prescription · solve → solution · compete → competition |
| ⚠️ Exceptions with -d | atten**d** → atten**tion** · inten**d** → inten**tion** |
| **-cian** = a person with a skill | musi**cian**, electri**cian**, techni**cian**, politi**cian**, opti**cian**, physi**cian**, mathemati**cian** (from *music, electric, technic(al), politics, optics*) |
| **-stion** = /stʃən/ | que**stion**, sugge**stion**, dige**stion**, exhau**stion** |

⚠️ **British spelling:** cancel → can**cell**ation (double *l*); pronounce → pron**u**nciation (no *o* after *n*).

#### B2. -al vs -el vs -le

| Ending | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **-al** | Most **adjectives**; nouns made from verbs; many common nouns | loc**al**, natur**al**, fin**al**, norm**al**, centr**al**, rur**al**, glob**al**, annu**al**, practic**al** ★, physic**al** ★, cultur**al** ★ · arriv**al**, approv**al**, propos**al**, surviv**al**, rent**al**, refus**al** · hospit**al**, anim**al**, mamm**al** ★, festiv**al**, sign**al**, met**al**, capit**al**, journ**al**, termin**al**, interv**al** |
| **-el** | Mostly **nouns**, especially after **m, n, r, v, w, ch, sh** and a soft **c / g** | cam**el**, tunn**el**, chann**el**, pan**el**, barr**el**, nov**el**, lev**el**, trav**el**, tow**el**, vow**el**, jew**el**, parc**el**, canc**el**, ang**el** · also hot**el**, mod**el**, lab**el**, host**el**, fu**el**, vess**el**, personn**el** |
| **-le** | The **most common** ending of all, especially after **b, d, k, p, t, g, z, f** | tab**le**, peop**le**, vehic**le**, artic**le**, bicyc**le**, circ**le**, tit**le**, midd**le**, simp**le**, samp**le**, examp**le**, sing**le**, doub**le**, coup**le**, bott**le**, puzz**le**, cab**le**, cast**le** |

⚠️ **Sound-alike pairs:** angle (90°) / angel (in heaven) · metal (iron) / medal (a prize) · principal (head of a school; main) / principle (a rule) · council (the local government) / counsel (advice) · moral (right and wrong) / morale (team spirit).

#### B3. -ic vs -ical

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| Some words have **-ic only** | academic, scientific, specific, basic, public, dramatic, realistic, athletic, domestic, fantastic, democratic, energetic |
| Some words have **-ical only** | practical ★, physical ★, chemical, medical, musical, typical, logical, critical, technical, biological, geographical, tropical, identical |
| **Both forms, different meanings** | **economic** (about the economy: *economic growth*) / **economical** (cheap to use: *an economical car*) · **historic** (very important in history: *a historic day*) / **historical** (about the past: *historical records*) · **electric** (powered by electricity: *an electric car*) / **electrical** (about electricity: *an electrical engineer*) · **classic** (excellent, typical: *a classic film*) / **classical** (traditional: *classical music*) |
| **Adverbs:** -ic + **ally** | basic → basic**ally** · specific → specific**ally** · scientific → scientific**ally** · dramatic → dramatic**ally** · automatic → automatic**ally** |

⚠️ **The adverb ending -ically is pronounced /ɪkli/, so the *al* is silent.** Don't write *basicly*. The **only** exception is **publicly**.

#### B4. -cial vs -tial

Both are pronounced /ʃl/. **Look at the base word.**

| Base word ends in… | Ending | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **-ence**, or **-t** | **-tial** | ess**ence** → essential · resid**ence** → residential · confid**ence** → confidential · influ**ence** → influential · sequ**ence** → sequential · par**t** → partial · presiden**t** → presidential · poten(cy) → potential |
| **-ce** (not -ence) | **-cial** | finan**ce** → financial · offi**ce** → official · commer**ce** → commercial · ra**ce** → racial · fa**ce** → facial · provin**ce** → provincial · benefi**t** → beneficial ⚠️ |
| No base word (learn them) | — | spe**cial**, so**cial**, cru**cial** · ini**tial**, spa**tial** |

⚠️ **Exceptions:** substance → substan**tial**, circumstance → circumstan**tial**, benefit → benefi**cial**.

#### B5. -ous vs -ious vs -eous vs -uous

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| **-ous** is the default; **drop the e** of the base word | fam**e** → famous ★ · nerv**e** → nervous ★ · adventur**e** → adventurous · plus danger**ous**, enorm**ous**, gener**ous**, numer**ous**, poison**ous**, mountain**ous** |
| Base word ends in **consonant + y** → **y becomes i** → **-ious** | var**y** → various · fur**y** → furious · glor**y** → glorious · myster**y** → mysterious · luxur**y** → luxurious · industr**y** → industrious · harmon**y** → harmonious · env**y** → envious |
| You hear **/ʃəs/** → **-cious, -tious, -xious** (and *-scious*) | deli**cious**, spa**cious**, pre**cious**, suspi**cious** · ambi**tious**, cau**tious**, nutri**tious** · an**xious** · con**scious** ★ |
| You hear **/iəs/** with no base word → **-ious** | prev**ious**, obv**ious**, ser**ious**, cur**ious**, var**ious** |
| **Keep the e** after soft **g** → **-eous** | courag**e** → courageous · outrag**e** → outrageous · advantag**e** → advantageous · also gorg**eous**, spontan**eous**, simultan**eous** |
| **-uous** after the /ju/ sound | contin**uous**, ambig**uous**, strenu**ous** |

#### B6. -ful vs -fully

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| The suffix **-ful has ONE l** | care**ful**, use**ful**, help**ful**, success**ful** ★, harm**ful**, peace**ful**, stress**ful**, power**ful**, colour**ful**, thank**ful** |
| **-ful + ly = -fully** (now two l's) | care**fully**, success**fully**, beauti**fully**, hope**fully**, grate**fully** |
| **y → i** before -ful | beaut**y** → beautiful · pit**y** → pitiful · plent**y** → plentiful |
| ⚠️ British single l | skil**ful**, wil**ful**, fulfil, fulfilment, enrol, enrolment, instalment (AmE: skillful, fulfill, enroll) |
| ⚠️ *great* ≠ *grate* | **grate**ful (not *greatful*) · **aw**ful (from *awe*, the e goes) |
| Plurals of -ful nouns | spoon**fuls**, cup**fuls**, hand**fuls**, mouth**fuls** (not *spoonsful*) |

#### B7. -or vs -our vs -er vs -ar

| Ending | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **-our** (BrE) | Common nouns of feeling or quality | colour, behaviour, neighbour, favour, favourite ★, flavour, honour, humour, labour, harbour, rumour, vapour, endeavour |
| ⚠️ **-our loses the u** before *-ous, -ary, -ation, -ise* | | hum**or**ous, glam**or**ous, lab**or**ious, hon**or**ary, vap**or**ise (but **keeps** it in col**our**ful, fav**our**ite, hon**our**able, behavi**our**al) |
| **-or** for people | verb ends in **-ate, -ct, -ss, -it** | operat**e** → operator · translat**e** → translator · administrat**e** → administrator · coordinat**e** → coordinator · dire**ct** → director · instru**ct** → instructor · condu**ct** → conductor · inspe**ct** → inspector · profe**ss** → professor · vis**it** → visitor · ed**it** → editor · plus doctor, author, tutor, sailor, supervisor, counsellor |
| **-er** for people | most everyday verbs | teach**er**, manag**er**, employ**er**, research**er**, design**er**, photograph**er**, build**er**, own**er**, speak**er**, lectur**er** |
| **-ar** | learn these (many are IELTS words) | semin**ar**, calend**ar**, gramm**ar** ★, simil**ar** ★, popul**ar**, regul**ar**, particul**ar**, famili**ar**, circul**ar**, sol**ar**, lun**ar**, pol**ar**, coll**ar**, cell**ar**, doll**ar**, pill**ar**, sug**ar**, guit**ar** ★, schol**ar**, regist**rar**, burgl**ar**, li**ar**, begg**ar** |

#### B8. -er vs -re (British English)

| Ending | Examples |
|---|---|
| **-re** (BrE) | **centre** (*city centre, sports centre, leisure centre, shopping centre*), **theatre**, **metre** (a measurement: *5 metres*), **litre**, **kilometre**, **fibre**, **sombre**, **manoeuvre**; also **acre**, **massacre**, **mediocre** (all spellings) |
| **-er** (all spellings) | water, letter, number, winter, computer, register, character, chapter, filter, order, offer, poster, member, minister, disaster, quarter, cylinder, river |
| ⚠️ **meter vs metre** | a **meter** measures something (*gas meter, parking meter*); a **metre** is 100 cm |
| Words made from -re | centre → **central** · theatre → **theatrical** · metre → **metric** · fibre → **fibrous** (the *e* moves or goes) |

#### B9. -ise vs -ize (and -yse)

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| British English accepts **both -ise and -ize** for most verbs. **Choose -ise** and use it always. | organise, realise, recognise, apologise, criticise, emphasise, specialise, minimise, prioritise, summarise, memorise, socialise, modernise |
| **Always -ise** (in both British and American English) | advertise, advise, arise, compromise, comprise, devise, disguise, enterprise, exercise ★, franchise, improvise, revise, supervise, surprise, televise |
| **Always -ize** (in both) | size, prize (*an award*), capsize, and **seize** (with *ei*) |
| **Always -yse** in British English | analyse, paralyse, catalyse (AmE: analyze) |

### Common mistakes and corrections

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| neccessary | **necessary** | one **c**, two **s** (one Collar, two Sleeves) |
| stationary (*for pens*) | **stationery** | **e** for **e**nvelopes |
| libary | **library** | li-**br**-ary: two *r*'s |
| temporery | **temporary** | adjective → -ary |
| cemetary | **cemetery** | three *e*'s: c-**e**-m-**e**-t-**e**-ry |
| entre ★ | **entry** | enter → entry (Rule 4) |
| conculation ★ | **conclusion** | conclu**de** → conclu**sion** (B1) |
| practicial ★ | **practical** | -ical only, no extra *i* (B3) |
| basicly | **basically** | -ic + **ally** (B3) |
| beautyful | **beautiful** | *y* → *i* (B6) |
| successfull ★ | **successful** | -ful has one *l* (B6) |
| grammer ★ / similer ★ / guiter ★ | **grammar / similar / guitar** | the -ar group (B7) |
| favourate ★ | **favourite** | favour + **ite** (B7) |
| famouse / femous ★ | **famous** | fam**e** → famous: drop the *e*, keep the *a* (B5) |
| excercise ★ | **exercise** | no *c* after the *x*; always **-ise** (B9) |

### Memory techniques

- 💡 **neCeSSary:** one **C**ollar and two **S**leeves.
- 💡 **stationEry has Envelopes**; **stationAry is At rest**.
- 💡 **A cEmEtEry has three Es**, like three gravestones.
- 💡 **-ary is the default, -ery needs a base word** (bake → bakery).
- 💡 **Hear /ʒən/? Write -sion.** *Vision, decision, television.*
- 💡 **"Basically" has an ALLY:** basic + **ally**.
- 💡 **-ful is never full:** only one *l*.
- 💡 **A seminAR in the cAR, a guitAR at the bAR:** the -ar words.
- 💡 **British -re: centre, theatre, metre.** The **R** comes before the **E**, as in **Royal English**.
