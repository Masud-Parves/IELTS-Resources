## [Day 7] Special Chapter: Singular and Plural Spelling Traps

### Learning objectives

By the end of today you will be able to:

- decide **before you listen** whether a gap needs a singular, plural or uncountable noun;
- **hear** plural endings, including the ones that almost disappear (*desks, costs, months*);
- spell **regular and irregular plurals** (*cities, shelves, potatoes, children, criteria*);
- recognise **33 nouns that are the same in the singular and the plural** (*sheep, species, aircraft*);
- never add **-s** to an **uncountable noun** (*information, equipment, accommodation, furniture*);
- write **compound adjectives** correctly (*a ten-minute walk*, not *a ten-minutes walk*).

> ⚠️ **In IELTS Listening, the number must be exactly right.** If the answer is *tickets* and you write *ticket*, you score **zero**. If the answer is *equipment* and you write *equipments*, you also score **zero**.

### Part 1: Hearing plurals

#### The three sounds of the plural -s

| Sound | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **/s/** | after a voiceless sound (/p/, /t/, /k/, /f/, /θ/) | maps, students, books, chefs, months |
| **/z/** | after a voiced sound (vowels and /b/, /d/, /ɡ/, /l/, /m/, /n/, /v/) | rooms, bags, fees, tables, hotels, cars |
| **/ɪz/** | after /s/, /z/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/, /dʒ/ (adds a syllable) | buses, classes, prizes, dishes, churches, pages, courses |
| **/iːz/** | Greek and Latin plurals of *-is* words | crises /ˈkraɪsiːz/, analyses /əˈnæləsiːz/, theses, bases /ˈbeɪsiːz/ |

#### Plurals that are hard to hear

| Problem | Examples | What you hear |
|---|---|---|
| **-sts, -sks, -sps** (three consonants together) | tests, costs, lists, tourists, scientists, guests, desks, tasks, risks | the last /s/ is very short or seems to disappear |
| **-ths, -cts, -pts** | months, lengths, facts, insects, products, receipts, concepts | the /θ/ or /t/ disappears: *"monss"*, *"facss"* |
| **Plural + a word starting with s** | *tourists stay*, *shops sell*, *bikes stolen* | the two /s/ sounds join into one |
| **Plural + a word starting with a vowel** | *books are*, *rooms in*, *cars on* | the /s/ or /z/ links to the next word: *"book-sare"* |

> 💡 **So don't rely on your ear alone.** Use the **grammar clues** in the question to decide, and then use your ear to confirm.

### Part 2: The grammar clues (your "number detector")

| Plural clue → add -s | Singular clue → no -s | Uncountable clue → never -s |
|---|---|---|
| a number above one: *two, 25, several* | *a / an*, *one*, *a single*, *the only* | *much*, *a little*, *less* |
| *many, a few, few, various, numerous* | *each, every, another* | *an amount of, a great deal of* |
| *a number of, a range of, a variety of, a selection of, a series of* | *either, neither* | *a piece of, an item of* |
| *both, these, those, all (the)* | *this, that* | (*some, any, a lot of, most* can go with plural **or** uncountable nouns) |
| *one of the …s*, *each of the …s* | *per* (*£5 per person*) | singular verb after the gap: *___ is provided* |
| plural verb after the gap: *___ are / were / have* | singular verb: *___ is / was / has* | |
| *a pair of*, *dozens of*, *hundreds of* | | |

⚠️ **Watch these two:**

- *a number of* students **are** … → plural noun (= *many*)
- *the number of* students **is** … → plural noun after *of*, singular verb (= *the amount*)

#### Compound adjectives: number + noun + noun → always singular

When a number and a noun come **before another noun**, the first noun **loses its -s** and you add a hyphen.

| Phrase | Compound adjective |
|---|---|
| a walk of **ten minutes** | a **ten-minute** walk |
| a course that lasts **three days** | a **three-day** course |
| a flat with **two bedrooms** | a **two-bedroom** flat |
| a hotel with **five stars** | a **five-star** hotel |
| a child who is **seven years** old | a **seven-year-old** child |
| a note worth **twenty pounds** | a **twenty-pound** note |
| a report of **20 pages** | a **20-page** report |

#### Nouns used as adjectives → usually singular

A noun that describes another noun usually has **no -s**: car park, bus stop, shoe shop, student card, ticket office, tooth**brush**, book club, team player ★ (not *teams player*), apple tree, phone number.

⚠️ **Exceptions** (these nouns are always plural, or the plural has a different meaning): **sports** centre, **clothes** shop, **savings** account, **sales** manager, **customs** officer, **arms** trade, **careers** advice, **goods** train.

#### Numbers and measurements

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Wrong | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| two **hundred** students | two hundreds students | *hundred, thousand, million, dozen* have no -s after a number |
| **hundreds of** students | hundred of students | …but they take -s in *hundreds of, thousands of* |
| six **months** | six month | a plain number + noun → plural |
| a six-**month** course | a six-months course | a compound adjective → singular |
| fifty **per cent** / percent | fifty per cents | no -s on *per cent* |
| **20 kg** / 20 **kilos** | 20 kgs | no -s on abbreviations |

### Part 3: How to spell regular plurals

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Most nouns: + s** | room → rooms · ticket → tickets · fee → fees |
| **-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z: + es** | bus → bus**es** · class → class**es** · dish → dish**es** · church → church**es** · box → box**es** · quiz → qui**zz**es |
| **consonant + y → ies** | city → cit**ies** · country → countr**ies** · facility → facilit**ies** · activity → activit**ies** · library → librar**ies** · university → universit**ies** · battery → batter**ies** · laboratory → laborator**ies** · allergy → allerg**ies** |
| **vowel + y: + s** | day → days · key → keys · holiday → holidays · essay → essays · journey → journeys · survey → surveys · valley → valleys · monkey → monkeys |
| **-f / -fe → ves** | knife → kni**ves** · life → li**ves** · wife → wi**ves** · half → hal**ves** · leaf → lea**ves** · shelf → shel**ves** · loaf → loa**ves** · thief → thie**ves** · wolf → wol**ves** · calf → cal**ves** |
| ⚠️ **-f / -fe: + s** (exceptions) | roof → roof**s** · chief → chief**s** · belief → belief**s** · cliff → cliff**s** · proof → proof**s** · chef → chef**s** · safe → safe**s** · giraffe → giraffe**s** · reef → reef**s** |
| **consonant + o → oes** | potato → potato**es** · tomato → tomato**es** · hero → hero**es** · echo → echo**es** · volcano → volcano**es** · mosquito → mosquito**es** |
| **-o: + s** (vowel + o, short forms, music, Spanish/Italian words) | photo**s** · piano**s** · radio**s** · video**s** · zoo**s** · kilo**s** · studio**s** · logo**s** · memo**s** · kangaroo**s** · tattoo**s** · avocado**s** · casino**s** · ratio**s** · scenario**s** · portfolio**s** · patio**s** |
| **Both accepted** | volcanoes / volcanos · mosquitoes / mosquitos · mangoes / mangos · zeros / zeroes · cargoes / cargos · tornadoes / tornados |

💡 **Potatoes and tomatoes have toes; photos and pianos don't.**

### Part 4: Irregular plurals

| Singular | Plural | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| man | **men** | foot | **feet** |
| woman /ˈwʊmən/ | **women** /ˈwɪmɪn/ | tooth | **teeth** |
| child | **children** | goose | **geese** |
| person | **people** | mouse | **mice** |
| ox | **oxen** | die | **dice** |
| businessman, postman | business**men**, post**men** | salesperson | sales**people** |

⚠️ **These look irregular but aren't:** German → German**s** · human → human**s** · Norman → Norman**s** (they're not compounds of *man*).

⚠️ **Compounds with "man/woman" as the first word make both parts plural:** a woman doctor → **women doctors**.

### Part 5: Nouns with the same singular and plural form (33)

> 💡 With these nouns, **the verb, the article or the number** tells you whether it is singular or plural. The spelling **never** changes, so never add -s.

| Group | Nouns | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Animals** | sheep · deer · fish · salmon · trout · cod · moose · bison · swine · reindeer · elk · squid | *one sheep, two hundred **sheep*** |
| **Offspring** | offspring | *The bird feeds its **offspring**.* |
| **Vehicles ending in -craft** | aircraft · spacecraft · hovercraft | *Three **aircraft** were delayed.* |
| **Words ending in -s** | species /ˈspiːʃiːz/ · series /ˈsɪəriːz/ · means · crossroads · headquarters · barracks · chassis /ˈʃæsi/ (pl. /ˈʃæsiz/) · corps /kɔː/ (pl. /kɔːz/) | *This **species** is rare. These **species** are rare.* |
| **Nationalities ending in -ese and -ss** | Swiss · Chinese · Japanese · Portuguese | *The **Japanese** are known for…* |
| **Numbers after a number** | dozen · hundred · thousand · million | *two **dozen** eggs, three **thousand** people* |
| **British weight** | stone | *He weighs 12 **stone**.* |

⚠️ ***Fishes*** exists, but only for **different types** of fish in science. In everyday English and IELTS answers, write **fish**.

### Part 6: Latin and Greek plurals (common in Sections 3 and 4)

| Pattern | Singular → Plural |
|---|---|
| **-is → -es** /iːz/ | analysis → analys**es** · basis → bas**es** · crisis → cris**es** · thesis → thes**es** · hypothesis → hypothes**es** · diagnosis → diagnos**es** · oasis → oas**es** · emphasis → emphas**es** · axis → ax**es** |
| **-on → -a** | phenomenon → phenomen**a** · criterion → criteri**a** |
| **-um → -a** | datum → dat**a** · medium → medi**a** · curriculum ★ → curricul**a** (or *curriculums*) · bacterium → bacteri**a** · millennium → millenni**a** · memorandum → memorand**a** · stratum → strat**a** |
| **-us → -i** | fungus → fung**i** · cactus → cact**i** · nucleus → nucle**i** · stimulus → stimul**i** · radius → radi**i** · alumnus → alumn**i** · syllabus → syllab**i** (or *syllabuses*) |
| **-a → -ae** | larva → larv**ae** · alga → alg**ae** · vertebra → vertebr**ae** · formula → formul**ae** (or *formulas*) · antenna → antenn**ae** (insects; for radios: *antennas*) |
| **-ix / -ex → -ices** | appendix → append**ices** · index → ind**ices** · matrix → matr**ices** (or *-ixes / -exes*) |
| ⚠️ **-us → -uses (ordinary English plural)** | campus → camp**uses** · virus → vir**uses** · bonus → bon**uses** · bus → bus**es** · census → cens**uses** · status → stat**uses** (*not* cami, viri) |

💡 **One criterion, many criteria. One phenomenon, many phenomena.** The **-on** is singular, and the **-a** is plural.

### Part 7: Nouns that are always plural

These have **no singular form** in their normal meaning. They take a **plural verb**, and you count them with **a pair of** or **some**.

| Group | Nouns |
|---|---|
| **Clothes and tools in two parts** (use *a pair of*) | trousers · jeans · shorts · pyjamas · tights · glasses · sunglasses · binoculars · scissors · pliers · headphones |
| **Things and places** | clothes · goods · belongings · valuables · groceries · surroundings · outskirts · premises · stairs · contents · remains · refreshments |
| **Money** | savings · earnings · funds (*in the sense of money*) |
| **Other** | congratulations · thanks · customs (*at the border*) · arms (*weapons*) · headquarters |

*My **glasses are** on the desk. Please take all your **belongings**. The **outskirts** of the city **are** quiet.*

### Part 8: Words ending in -s that are singular

| Group | Nouns | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **News** | news | *The **news is** good.* (never *a news*: use *a piece of news*) |
| **School subjects in -ics** | physics · mathematics · economics · statistics · politics · linguistics · electronics · athletics · gymnastics | ***Economics is** my favourite subject.* |
| **Illnesses** | measles · diabetes · mumps | ***Diabetes is** common.* |
| **Games** | billiards · darts · dominoes | ***Darts is** popular in pubs.* |

⚠️ When *statistics* means **numbers**, it is plural: *These statistics show a rise.*

### Part 9: Uncountable nouns (never add -s)

These are the **most common IELTS trap**. Learn this list.

| Group | Uncountable nouns |
|---|---|
| **Information and study** | information · advice · knowledge · research · homework · schoolwork ★ · coursework · feedback · evidence · progress · training · education · assistance · guidance · permission · software |
| **Things** | equipment · furniture · luggage · baggage · machinery · stationery · jewellery · clothing · cutlery · crockery · hardware · rubbish · litter · money · cash · post / mail |
| **Places and systems** | **accommodation** (in British English) · traffic · transport · parking · housing · scenery · wildlife · vegetation |
| **Nature** | weather · pollution · damage · electricity · water · air · oxygen · sunshine · rain · snow · fog |
| **Activities and ideas** | work · travel · employment · unemployment · insurance · leisure · fun · health · music · luck · behaviour |
| **Food** | bread · rice · pasta · meat · cheese · milk · butter · sugar · fruit (usually) |

#### How to count an uncountable noun

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---|---|
| an advice, two advices | **a piece of** advice, **some** advice |
| an information | **a piece of** information, **some** information |
| two furnitures | **two pieces of** furniture |
| three luggages | **three pieces of** luggage / **three bags** |
| an equipment | **a piece of** equipment |
| a news | **a piece of** news |
| a bread | **a loaf of** bread / **a slice of** bread |
| a clothing | **an item of** clothing / **a piece of** clothing |
| a research | **a piece of** research / **a research project** |
| a travel | **a trip** / **a journey** |

⚠️ ***Accommodation*** is uncountable in British English (*accommodations* is American). If the answer is *accommodation*, write it **without -s**.

### Part 10: Countable and uncountable, with different meanings

| Uncountable | Countable |
|---|---|
| **paper** (the material) | **a paper** (a newspaper; an academic article) · **papers** (documents) |
| **work** (a job, effort) | **a work** of art · **roadworks**, **gasworks** (places) |
| **experience** (knowledge from doing) | **experiences** (things that happened to you) |
| **time** | **three times** (occasions) |
| **room** (space) | **rooms** (in a building) |
| **glass** (the material) | **a glass** (for drinking) · **glasses** (spectacles) |
| **iron** (the metal) | **an iron** (for clothes) |
| **business** (trade) | **businesses** (companies) |
| **hair** (all of it) | **two hairs** (single ones) |
| **coffee / tea** (the drink) | **two coffees** (cups of coffee) |
| **chicken** (meat) | **chickens** (birds) |
| **competition** (rivalry) | **competitions** (contests) |
| **space** (the universe; room) | **parking spaces** |

### Part 11: Collective nouns

| Noun | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **staff** | no -s for people; count with *members of staff* | *two **members of staff*** (not *two staffs*) |
| **police** | always plural verb | *The **police are** coming.* |
| **cattle** | always plural, no -s | *The **cattle are** in the field.* |
| **people** | plural of *person* | ***People are** waiting.* (*peoples* = nations, rare) |
| **team, family, government, committee, audience, crew, class** | singular or plural verb in British English | *The **team is / are** playing well.* |
| **livestock, poultry** | plural meaning, no -s | *The farm sells **livestock**.* |

### Part 12: Compound nouns and possessives

| Type | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **noun + preposition/phrase** | the **main** noun takes -s | passer**s**-by · mother**s**-in-law · editor**s**-in-chief · runner**s**-up |
| **verb + particle** | the **end** takes -s | grown-up**s** · check-in**s** · take-off**s** · drop-out**s** |
| **one-word compounds** | the **end** takes -s | toothbrush**es** · bookshel**ves** · housewi**ves** · spoonful**s** |
| **Possessive, one owner** | 's | a **student's** card (one student) |
| **Possessive, plural owners** | s' | the **students'** common room (many students) |
| **Possessive, irregular plural** | 's | **children's** playground · **women's** clothing · **people's** opinions |

⚠️ ***its*** = belonging to it; ***it's*** = it is / it has. *The museum changed **its** opening hours. **It's** open until 6.*

### Part 13: The IELTS decision checklist

Use these six steps for every gap in Sections 1–4.

1. **Read around the gap** before the audio starts: the words before **and** after it.
2. **Find the clue:** a number, *a/an*, *several*, *a pair of*, a verb (*is/are*) or a hyphen (*a ___-minute walk*).
3. **Ask: is the noun uncountable?** If it is on the Part 9 list, it can **never** take -s.
4. **Predict** the form and write a small **s?** next to the gap if you expect a plural.
5. **Listen** for /s/, /z/, /ɪz/ or /iːz/. If what you hear doesn't fit the grammar, you probably misheard. The correct answer always fits the sentence.
6. **When you transfer your answers**, check every ending: **-s, -es, -ies, -ves**.

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| informations, advices, equipments | **information, advice, equipment** | uncountable (Part 9) |
| accommodations | **accommodation** | uncountable in British English |
| furnitures, luggages | **furniture, luggage** | uncountable |
| schoolworks ★, homeworks | **schoolwork, homework** | uncountable |
| a ten-minutes walk | **a ten-minute walk** | compound adjective (Part 2) |
| teams player ★ | **team player** | noun used as an adjective |
| two hundreds people | **two hundred people** | no -s after a number |
| sheeps, deers, aircrafts | **sheep, deer, aircraft** | same form (Part 5) |
| a criteria, a phenomena | **a criterion, a phenomenon** | *-a* is the plural (Part 6) |
| potatos, tomatos | **potatoes, tomatoes** | consonant + o → oes |
| photoes, pianoes | **photos, pianos** | short forms and music words → os |
| shelfs, knifes | **shelves, knives** | -f/-fe → ves |
| rooves | **roofs** | exception: + s |
| citys, facilitys | **cities, facilities** | consonant + y → ies |
| holidaies, journies | **holidays, journeys** | vowel + y → + s |
| childs, peoples | **children, people** | irregular |
| the news are | **the news is** | *news* is singular |
| mamals ★ | **mammals** | double m; keep the -s when the answer is plural |
