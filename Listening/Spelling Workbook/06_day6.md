## [Day 6] IELTS Listening Vocabulary Bank

### Learning objectives

By the end of today you will be able to:

- spell **120 high-frequency IELTS Listening words** in the 10 topic areas that appear again and again in Sections 1–4;
- recognise the **spelling trap** in each word (the ❌ column shows the usual mistake);
- link today's words to the **rules from Days 1–5**;
- check an answer sheet the way an examiner does.

> 💡 **How to study 120 words in one day:** do **not** try to learn them all at once. Read one topic (12 words), **cover the Word column**, read the meaning, and write the word. Tick the words you got right. Then go to the next topic. At the end, go back **only** to the words you didn't tick.

> ⚠️ Words already taught on Days 1–5 are **not repeated** here. They are listed under each topic as **"Also from Days 1–5"**, so revise them too.
