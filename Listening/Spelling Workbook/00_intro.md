## [Start] How to Use This Workbook

### Why your spelling goes wrong in Listening

You usually **hear** the word correctly. The mistake happens in the 2 seconds between hearing it and writing it. There are four main reasons:

1. **The weak vowel /ə/ (schwa).** In English, unstressed syllables are reduced to a short, lazy /ə/ sound. import**ant** and differ**ent** both end in /ənt/, and perform**ance** and exist**ence** both end in /əns/. **Your ear cannot tell you the letter**, so you need a rule, a word family or a memory trick instead.
2. **Letters that are written but not pronounced:** silent letters (recei**p**t, We**d**nesday) and double consonants (a**cc**o**mm**odation), which sound exactly like single ones.
3. **Plural endings that disappear in fast speech.** In *"two desks"* the final /s/ is almost swallowed, and in *"tourists stay"* the two /s/ sounds merge into one. You hear one book but the answer is *books*.
4. **Words that sound the same but are spelt differently:** *patients / patience*, *stationery / stationary*, *weather / whether*.

This workbook gives you a **rule, a word family or a memory trick** for each of these. With those, you stop guessing.

### IELTS Listening spelling rules you must know

> ⚠️ **A wrongly spelt answer scores zero**, even if you heard it perfectly. A missing or extra plural **-s** also scores zero.

- **British or American spelling** is accepted (*colour* or *color*). Choose one and stay consistent. This workbook uses **British** spelling.
- **Capital letters** don't matter on the answer sheet. On paper, writing every answer in CAPITALS is allowed and can make your letters clearer.
- A **hyphenated word** counts as one word: *ten-minute* = 1 word.
- **Numbers** can be written as figures: *15* is safer than *fifteen*.
- **Words given in the question are spelt correctly for you.** If the answer word is printed in the question paper, copy it from there.
- When you **transfer answers** (paper test: 10 minutes; computer test: 2 minutes to check), check every answer letter by letter, and check its ending: **-s, -es, -ed, -ing, -ly**.

### The daily routine

| Step | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Learn | 20 min | Read the rules. Say every word in the table **aloud, syllable by syllable**. |
| 2. Look-Say-Cover-Write-Check | 15 min | For each word you don't know: look, say, cover, write from memory, check letter by letter. |
| 3. Exercises | 20 min | Do the written exercises **without looking back**. Mark them with the Answer Key. |
| 4. Dictation | 10 min | Play the day's audio file. Write each word once. Mark it. |
| 5. Assessment | 10 min | Do the daily assessment. Record your score in the tracker at the back. |
| 6. Real answer words | 10 min | Learn today's block from the **Real Answer Words** chapter and play its dictation. |

**Audio files** are in `Listening/Spelling Workbook/audio/` (Day1_Dictation.wav … Final_Test_Dictation.wav, and AnswerWords_Block1–7.wav). Each lesson word is read with a short sentence, and then there is a pause for you to write. Don't look at the word list while you listen. If you have no audio, ask a friend to read the dictation script from the Answer Key.

**Spaced review:** test every word you got wrong again **after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days**. A word is learnt when you spell it correctly in all three tests. Write your wrong words in the **Error Log** at the back of this workbook, and add any Listening mistakes to section 8 of your Spelling Fix List.
