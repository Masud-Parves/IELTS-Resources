## 2. Reusable Sentences

### Opening
- *I'd like to talk about [my uncle], who [works as a teacher].*
- *There are many people I could talk about, but the first person who comes to mind is [my best friend, Rafi].*

### Who the person is
- *[He]'s in [his late twenties] and works as [an engineer] in [Dhaka].*
- *[She]'s my [aunt] on my [mother's] side.*

### How we met
- *We first met [at school], and we've known each other for [ten years].*
- *I got to know [him] through [a friend].*

### Personality + example
- *What I like most about [him] is that [he]'s really [reliable]. For example, [...].*
- *[She]'s the kind of person who [always helps others].*
- *[He]'s not only [intelligent] but also [very humble].*

### What they do
- *Every [morning], [he] [goes for a jog].*
- *[She] spends most of [her] free time [gardening].*

### A memory
- *I still remember one time when [...].*

### Feelings
- *I really admire [him] because [he never gives up].*
- *[She]'s taught me that [...].*

### Closing
- *That's why [he] means so much to me.*
- *I hope I can be more like [her] one day.*

### Famous people
- *I've never met [him] in person, but I've followed [his] career for years.*
- *If I ever met [him], I'd ask [him] [...].*

### My sentences
- *Truly dedicated and promising [teachers or friends] are quite rare nowadays.*
- *He has a rare combination of confidence and humility, which makes people respect him even more.*
- *There is something incredibly magnetic about his personality that naturally draws people toward him.*
- *I found that the way he understands [tour planning] (the thing he is best at), no one else does.*
- *He taught me a lot of facts that are crucial to fighting with the concrete reality of life.*
- *In every bad period of my life, he stood beside me like a deep-rooted tree.* (past)
- *In every bad period of my life, he always supports me like a deep-rooted tree.* (present)
- *During difficult times in his business, he stood beside his company like a deep-rooted tree.*
- *When I see him, I feel like I'm over the moon; he is like a breath of fresh air in my hectic lifestyle.*
- *He inspires me in every aspect of my life.*

## 3. Cue Cards (Makkar, 33 cards)

#### Card 3 · Describe a person you know who has healthy habits and a healthy daily routine

**You should say:**

- Who this person is
- How you got to know each other
- What he/she does to keep this lifestyle
- And explain how you feel about him/her

> **My answer:**
>
> **Well,** I would like to talk about my best friend, Rishad, who always leads a healthy lifestyle and sticks to a healthy daily routine. He is extremely diligent and passionate about health and exercise. Truly dedicated and health-conscious people like him are quite rare nowadays.
>
> We got to know each other at university, as we were in the same class. We soon became close friends, and since then, he has been my best friend. I still remember that in our hostel days, he used to wake me up early for a morning walk.
>
> **As for his daily routine,** he wakes up early every morning, and he walks at least 8,000 steps. **Besides,** he does different kinds of exercise to keep his body fit. He is also very punctual and conscious of his physical and mental health. **For example,** he practises meditation every day to keep his mind fresh. He also eats a balanced diet and avoids junk food.
>
> **Honestly,** I feel lucky to have a friend like him. He always motivates us to take care of our bodies and minds. In every bad period of my life, he stood beside me like a deep-rooted tree. **That is why** he inspires me in every aspect of my life, and I hope I can be more like him one day.

#### Card 6 · Describe a person you only met once recently and want to know more about

**You should say:**

- Who is he/she
- When you met him/her
- Why do you want to know more about him/her
- And explain how you feel about him/her

> **My answer:**
>
> **Well,** I would like to talk about Sahin Khan, a friend of one of my friends, whom I met only once, last month. He is currently studying music at Dhaka University, and he is very good at singing and playing the guitar. Truly dedicated music lovers like him are quite rare nowadays.
>
> I met him at a small get-together at my friend's house. He is a very sociable person, so when we met, I did not feel that it was our first meeting. My friend told me that he is also funny and reliable, and for this reason, everyone in his friend circle loves him a lot. During our first meeting, I found that the way he understands the beat, melody and rhythm of music, no one else in our group does.
>
> He is extremely diligent and passionate about music. **In fact,** he even left his MBBS course to study music. **Besides,** he is a career-oriented person who keeps improving himself. He shared that he won several music competitions at his university's cultural programmes. He is very dedicated to his music, and he even writes his own songs. **For example,** he told us that he once took almost two years to complete a single song.
>
> **That is why** I want to know more about him. **Actually,** I am really curious why he left a medical career for music, because it was a big and brave decision. I would also like to learn how he writes his songs, and maybe he can teach me a few guitar chords.
>
> **Honestly,** I feel inspired by his courage, because he followed his passion. I have met him only once, **but** that meeting was like a breath of fresh air in my hectic lifestyle, and I hope we will meet again soon.
