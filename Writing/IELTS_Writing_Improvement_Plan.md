## 5. My Personal Checklist

These come from **my own essays**, not from a general list.

### A. Task Response (check while planning)

- [ ] My opinion sentence says **which view**: *I completely support **the latter** view because ___.*
- [ ] **My view goes last** in the intro. If I support the first view in the question, I swap the order.
- [ ] **One reason**, the same in the intro, the BP2 topic sentence and the conclusion.
- [ ] BP2 starts with ***I firmly believe***, not *critics argue* or *supporters believe*.
- [ ] No *also, both, equally, balance, not only… but also* in my opinion sentences.
- [ ] Examples start with ***a ___ who ___***, never *if any student*.
- [ ] The example shows **the sentence before it** (the 5-second test), and doesn't drift to leadership or creativity.
- [ ] BP2 has an **R sentence**: *Although some people argue that [BP1 point], [my side].*

### B. Grammar (check in the last 5 minutes)

| # | My pattern | ❌ I wrote | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | After *should/will/can*: base form or passive | should not **gives** to children | should not **be given** to children |
| 2 | Subject–verb agreement | if a student **study** | if a student **studies** |
| 3 | Word forms | leads to **successful** · should **consistence** · **equal** important | leads to **success** · stay **consistent** · **equally** important |
| 4 | Never start a sentence with *Which* | **Which** later led to… | **This** can later lead to… |
| 5 | No comma between two sentences | …activities**, other** believe · **, therefore** students | …activities**, while others** believe · **so that** students |
| 6 | Articles | finish syllabus · creative mind | finish **the** syllabus · **a** creative mind |
| 7 | Fixed phrases | participate **on** · **by** this way · **less** or no · **in** time | participate **in** · **in** this way · **little** or no · **on** time |
| 8 | Uncountable nouns, so no -s | schoolwork**s** · exercise**s** (activity) · leadership**s** | schoolwork · exercise · leadership |

### C. Mechanics (check in the last 5 minutes)

- [ ] A space after every full stop (*life. Therefore*).
- [ ] No contractions (*it's* → *it is*).
- [ ] No *he/she*; use *they* or plural nouns.
- [ ] Spelling: **extracurricular**, **every day** (how often), **career**. The full list is in [IELTS_Spelling_Fix_List.md](IELTS_Spelling_Fix_List.md).
- [ ] Word count 260–299.

## 6. The Routine for Every Essay

1. **Plan (5 min):** type, side, one reason and one example for **each** view.
2. **Write (about 30 min).**
3. **Check (5 min):** Checklists B and C, reading **from the last sentence to the first**.
4. **Send it to Claude** for a review.
5. **Rewrite (next day, 20 min):** rewrite from my plan without looking at the corrected version, then compare. *The rewrite is where most of the learning happens.*
6. **Log:** new spelling errors go to the spelling list, and the scores go into the tracker below.

**Time:** about 1 hour on essay days and 30 minutes on rewrite days. This leaves time for Listening and Speaking.

## 8. If I Get Stuck in the Exam

| Problem | What I do |
|---|---|
| No idea for a view | Use the simplest idea I can explain: *money, time, health, safety, skills*. |
| Not sure how to spell a word | Use a word I can spell (*beneficial* → *helpful*). |
| 5 minutes left and no conclusion | Write the 2-sentence conclusion now. A missing conclusion costs more than one weak sentence. |
| Over 300 words | Leave it. Use the time to check grammar instead. |
