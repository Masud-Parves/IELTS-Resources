### The PEEL(R) paragraph method

| Label | Meaning | What the sentence does | Typical linkers |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span class="pl pl-P">Point</span> | Topic sentence | States the main idea of the paragraph | The main reason… / Firstly… / However, I believe… |
| <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span> | Explanation | Says **why** or **how** the point is true (often 2–3 sentences) | This is because… / Since… / As a result… |
| <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span> | Example | Gives a specific, realistic example | For example… / For instance… / In Bangladesh… |
| <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span> | Link / result | Connects back to the question or shows the result | Therefore… / As a result… / In this way… |
| <span class="pl pl-R">Response</span> | Response to the other side | Admits the opposite view, then answers it (the **R** in PEELR) | Admittedly… / Although… / However, … |

<div class="tip" markdown="1">

**PEEL বনাম PEELR:** সাধারণ body paragraph-এ **PEEL** যথেষ্ট। কিন্তু opinion বা discussion essay-তে যখন বিপরীত মতকে মেনে নিয়ে জবাব দিতে হয় ("Admittedly… However…"), তখন **R (Response)** যোগ করো — এতে লেখা আরও পরিণত ও যুক্তিপূর্ণ দেখায়, যা Band 7-এর জন্য গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

</div>

<div class="tip" markdown="1">

**Agree / Disagree essay (7টি) — নতুন নির্দিষ্ট কাঠামো** (IELTS_Agree_Disagree_Essay_Guide.md অনুযায়ী):

- 🚫 **কখনো আংশিক উত্তর নয়:** শুরু থেকে শেষ পর্যন্ত একটিই পক্ষ। "partly agree", "largely agree", "to some extent" লিখবে না।
- **Introduction (2টি বাক্য):** প্রশ্নের paraphrase → I completely agree/disagree with this view because + Idea 1 + Idea 2।
- **Body 1 = Idea 1:** <span class="pl pl-P">Point</span> → <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span> → <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span> → <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span> → <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>।
- **Body 1 linker:** The main reason why I agree/disagree with this idea is that… → This is because… → "; as a result," → For example… → Therefore…।
- **Body 2 = Idea 2:** <span class="pl pl-P">Point</span> → <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span> → <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span> → <span class="pl pl-R">Response</span> → <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>।
- **Body 2 linker:** Another important reason for my agreement/disagreement is that… → When… → For instance… → Although some people argue that…, [তোমার পক্ষ] → Consequently…।
- **Conclusion (2টি বাক্য):** In conclusion, I firmly believe that… → Idea 1 ও Idea 2 নতুন শব্দে। নতুন কোনো idea নয়।
- ⭐ **Response বাক্য:** বিপরীত মত থাকবে Although অংশে, আর তোমার পক্ষ থাকবে comma-র পরে, but ছাড়া। তাই উত্তর আংশিক হয় না।

</div>

<div class="tip" markdown="1">

**Discuss Both Views essay (7টি) — নতুন নির্দিষ্ট কাঠামো** (IELTS_Discuss_Both_Views_Essay_Guide.md অনুযায়ী):

- 🚫 **কখনো আংশিক উত্তর নয়:** একটিই স্পষ্ট মত। "both views are important", "it depends" লিখবে না।
- ⭐ **Order rule:** বিপরীত মত সবসময় আগে, তোমার মত সবসময় শেষে। তাই "the latter view" সবসময় তোমার পক্ষকেই বোঝায়।
- **Introduction:** While some people argue that [বিপরীত মত], others believe that [তোমার মত]. → I completely support the latter view because [মূল কারণ].
- **Body 1 = বিপরীত মত ("I" ছাড়া):** <span class="pl pl-P">Point</span> → <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span> → <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span> → <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span> → <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>।
- **Body 1 linker:** On the one hand, people who… argue that… → For example… → For this reason, many people believe that…।
- **Body 2 = তোমার মত:** <span class="pl pl-P">Point</span> → <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span> → <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span> → <span class="pl pl-R">Response</span> → <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>।
- **Body 2 linker:** On the other hand, I firmly believe that… because… → For instance… → Although some people argue that [Body 1-এর যুক্তি], [তোমার পক্ষ] → Consequently…।
- **Conclusion (2টি বাক্য):** In conclusion, people hold different views on whether… → While [বিপরীত মত], I firmly believe that [তোমার মত] because [কারণ]।
- **শব্দসংখ্যা:** সবসময় 300-এর নিচে। সহজ হিসাব: 40 + 100 + 100 + 40 = 280।

</div>

### 1. GOV-01 — Lifestyle decisions: government or individuals?

<div class="question">Should governments make decisions about people's lifestyle, or should people make their own decisions?</div>

| Plan | |
|---|---|
| **Essay type** | Discussion (both views + opinion) |
| **Position** | Individuals should make their own lifestyle decisions. |
| **Ideas from Idea Bank (Part 3)** | The Role of Government: Laws, Lifestyle and Individual Freedom (the Health sheet has a second, health-focused answer to this question) |
| **Structure** | Discuss Both Views guide: Both views + clean opinion → BP1 the other view (P E E X L) → BP2 my view (P E X R L) → Both themes + opinion in new words |
| **Reused from Idea Bank** | 4 sentences (highlighted) |
| **Word count** | Intro 36 · BP1 92 · BP2 114 · Conclusion 41 · **Total 283** |

</div>

<div class="model" markdown="1">

<p class="plabel">Introduction <span class="wc">· 36 words</span></p>

While some people argue that governments should control how citizens live, others believe that these decisions belong to individuals. I completely support the latter view because adults have the right to run their own private lives.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 1 <span class="wc">· 92 words</span></p>

<span class="pl pl-P">Point</span>On the one hand, people who support government action argue that <span class="reuse">laws can also protect people from harm</span>. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span><span class="reuse">Seat-belt laws, speed limits, bans on smoking in public places and food safety rules have saved many lives.</span> <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span>Dangerous behaviour can be controlled by clear rules; as a result, the whole of society becomes safer. <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span>For example, a driver who is fined for not wearing a seat belt may wear one every day, and this can save their life in an accident. <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>For this reason, many people believe that governments should make lifestyle decisions.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 2 <span class="wc">· 114 words</span></p>

<span class="pl pl-P">Point</span>On the other hand, I firmly believe that individuals should decide because <span class="reuse">adults should have the **right to make their own decisions** about their private lives, as long as they do not harm others</span>. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span><span class="reuse">If governments decide what people eat, wear or do in their free time, citizens may feel that their freedom is being taken away, and they may lose trust in their leaders.</span> <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span>For instance, a person who is told what clothes to wear may start to resent the government, which can damage public trust. <span class="pl pl-R">Response</span>Although some people argue that laws save lives, these rules are about public safety, not private lifestyle choices. <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>Consequently, private decisions should be left to people themselves.

<p class="plabel">Conclusion <span class="wc">· 41 words</span></p>

In conclusion, people hold different views on whether lifestyle decisions should be made by governments or by individuals. While road safety laws can save lives, I firmly believe that people should make these decisions because freedom is essential in private life.

</div>

<div class="question">A government has a responsibility to its citizens to ensure their safety. Therefore, some people think that the government should increase spending on defence but spend less on social benefits. To what extent do you agree?</div>

| **Position** | I completely disagree. |
| **Ideas from Idea Bank (Part 3)** | Education versus Defence · Welfare and Poverty |
| **Structure** | Agree/Disagree guide: Opinion + 2 ideas → BP1 Idea 1 (P E E X L) → BP2 Idea 2 (P E X R L) → Opinion + 2 ideas in new words |
| **Reused from Idea Bank** | 6 sentences (highlighted) |
| **Word count** | Intro 42 · BP1 116 · BP2 117 · Conclusion 38 · **Total 313** |

</div>

<div class="model" markdown="1">

<p class="plabel">Introduction <span class="wc">· 42 words</span></p>

It is sometimes claimed that, because governments must keep citizens safe, they should spend more on the military and less on social benefits. I completely disagree with this view because social benefits also protect people and excessive military spending is often wasteful.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 1 <span class="wc">· 116 words</span></p>

<span class="pl pl-P">Point</span>The main reason why I disagree with this idea is that social benefits also protect people. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span>This is because <span class="reuse">the main argument for welfare is that it acts as a **safety net**</span>, and <span class="reuse">people can lose their jobs, become seriously ill or suffer accidents through no fault of their own</span>. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span><span class="reuse">Without support, they may fall into deep poverty, lose their homes or be unable to feed their children</span>; as a result, many citizens would face daily danger. <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span>For example, a worker who loses their job can still feed their family while they retrain, and this support can prevent a temporary problem from becoming a disaster. <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>Therefore, social benefits make citizens safer, just as an army does.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 2 <span class="wc">· 117 words</span></p>

<span class="pl pl-P">Point</span>Another important reason for my disagreement is that excessive military spending is often wasteful. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span>When governments buy more weapons than they need, they lose money for public services, as <span class="reuse">modern weapons are extremely expensive, and money spent on them cannot be used for schools, hospitals or infrastructure</span>. <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span>For instance, a country with no immediate threat may buy new fighter planes while village hospitals have no medicine, which does little to protect ordinary families. <span class="pl pl-R">Response</span>Although some people argue that <span class="reuse">a government's **first responsibility** is to protect its citizens</span>, a larger army cannot protect them from hunger, illness or unemployment. <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>Consequently, <span class="reuse">defence spending should be sufficient to protect the country</span> and should not grow at the cost of welfare.

<p class="plabel">Conclusion <span class="wc">· 38 words</span></p>

In conclusion, I firmly believe that governments should not cut welfare in order to pay for more armed forces. Financial support keeps vulnerable people secure, and large weapons budgets also take money away from more useful public services.

</div>

<p class="plabel">Introduction <span class="wc">· 37 words</span></p>

While some people argue that the government is to blame for rising obesity among children, others believe that parents are at fault. I completely support the latter view because parents decide what their children eat every day.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 1 <span class="wc">· 100 words</span></p>

<span class="pl pl-P">Point</span>On the one hand, people who blame the government argue that it controls the environment in which children grow up. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span><span class="reuse">In some cities, there are few safe parks or playgrounds, so parents prefer to keep their children indoors.</span> <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span><span class="reuse">Many children spend hours playing video games instead of playing outside, and they are surrounded by advertisements for snacks and soft drinks.</span> <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span>For example, if junk food can be advertised freely during cartoons, children will keep asking for it, and this makes healthy eating harder for every family. <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>For this reason, many people believe that the government is responsible for childhood obesity.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 2 <span class="wc">· 102 words</span></p>

<span class="pl pl-P">Point</span>On the other hand, I firmly believe that parents are responsible because <span class="reuse">parents decide what their children eat at home</span>. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span>When children are young, they cannot make informed decisions, so they eat the food that adults provide. <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span>For instance, a child who is given fried snacks and soft drinks at home every day is likely to gain weight, whatever the government does. <span class="pl pl-R">Response</span>Although some people argue that advertising and the lack of parks are to blame, <span class="reuse">parents can prepare home-cooked meals, limit screen time, encourage outdoor play and act as role models</span>. <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>Consequently, parents hold the real responsibility for their children's weight.

<p class="plabel">Conclusion <span class="wc">· 42 words</span></p>

In conclusion, people hold different views on whether governments or parents are to blame for rising obesity among children. While advertising and a lack of parks make healthy living harder, I firmly believe that parents are responsible because they control daily meals.

</div>

<div class="question">The government should lower the budget for the arts in order to allocate more money to education. To what extent do you agree?</div>

It is sometimes claimed that the government should cut its arts budget and move this money to schools and universities. I completely disagree with this view because the arts budget is too small to help education and cutting it would harm culture and learning.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 1 <span class="wc">· 119 words</span></p>

<span class="pl pl-P">Point</span>The main reason why I disagree with this idea is that the arts budget is too small to make a real difference to education. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span>This is because arts funding is usually a very small part of public spending, while schools and universities need very large sums of money. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span>Moving this money would not pay for many teachers or buildings; as a result, education would gain little and the arts would lose almost everything. <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span>For example, the cost of running a national theatre for a year might repair only a few village schools, and this would do little for an entire school system. <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>Therefore, governments should look for this money in other places, such as by reducing waste and corruption.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 2 <span class="wc">· 107 words</span></p>

<span class="pl pl-P">Point</span>Another important reason for my disagreement is that cutting the arts budget would harm culture and learning. <span class="pl pl-E">Explain</span>When public support disappears, culture suffers, because <span class="reuse">many valuable art forms, especially traditional ones such as folk music, crafts and classical dance, cannot survive on ticket sales alone</span>. <span class="pl pl-X">Example</span>For instance, a folk dance group that earns little from tickets may close without public money, which would also end its free performances for local schoolchildren. <span class="pl pl-R">Response</span>Although some people argue that <span class="reuse">education is the **foundation of long-term development**</span>, creative skills are an important part of that education. <span class="pl pl-L">Link</span>Consequently, reducing arts funding would damage the very thing that it is supposed to improve.

<p class="plabel">Conclusion <span class="wc">· 40 words</span></p>

In conclusion, I firmly believe that taking money from the arts is the wrong way to pay for education. The amount saved would be too small to help schools, and the loss would also damage national culture and children's development.

### 5. GOV-06 — Can only governments change society?

<div class="question">Some people think that only the government can make significant changes in society, while others think that individuals can have a lot of influence. What is your opinion?</div>

| Plan | |
|---|---|
| **Essay type** | Discussion (both views + opinion) |
| **Position** | Individuals can have a great influence on society. |
| **Ideas from Idea Bank (Part 3)** | The Role of Government: Laws, Lifestyle and Individual Freedom · Museums, Libraries and Heritage (the Language Movement) |
| **Structure** | Discuss Both Views guide: Both views + clean opinion → BP1 the other view (P E E X L) → BP2 my view (P E X R L) → Both themes + opinion in new words |
| **Reused from Idea Bank** | 5 sentences (highlighted) |
| **Word count** | Intro 36 · BP1 96 · BP2 103 · Conclusion 42 · **Total 277** |

<div class="model" markdown="1">

<p class="plabel">Introduction <span class="wc">· 36 words</span></p>

While some people argue that only governments can bring about real change in society, others believe that individuals can be very influential. I completely support the latter view because many important changes began with ordinary people.

<p class="plabel">Body Paragraph 1 <span class="wc">· 96 words</span></p>
