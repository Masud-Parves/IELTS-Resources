# IELTS Writing — My Spelling Fix List

> **Goal:** stop repeating the same mistakes, so they do not hold back Lexical Resource.
> **Good news:** band 7 still allows *occasional* spelling errors. You do not need perfect spelling, only fewer repeated errors, especially in your key topic words.
> **Daily time:** 10 minutes, 5 words.

## 2. My Mistake Patterns

Most of your errors belong to a few patterns. Learn the pattern and you fix many words at once.

| Pattern | Rule | Words to practise |
|---|---|---|
| **Weak vowels** (you write *a* or *u* for *e*) | Say the word slowly, syllable by syllable: *sen-tence*, *ner-vous* | sentence, nervous, hesitate, separate, definitely |
| **-ar endings** | These sound like *-er* but end in *-ar* | similar, grammar, popular, regular, particular |
| **Missing or swapped letters** | Find the smaller word inside the big word | state-ment, para-phrase, out-weigh, en-viron-ment |
| **-ful has one l** | Always one l at the end | successful, useful, helpful, harmful, careful |
| **y becomes i** | Consonant + y → i before an ending | easy → easier, country → countries, study → studies |
| **Silent e goes before -ing** | Drop the e | make → making, write → writing, live → living |
| **Short vowel doubles the last letter** | stop → sto**pp**ing | plan → planning, get → getting, begin → beginning |

## 5. The 10-Minute Daily Routine

**Choose 5 words: 3 new words and 2 old words you got wrong before.** Then, for each word:

1. **Look** at the word and find the difficult part.
2. **Say** it slowly, in syllables: *con-ven-i-ent*.
3. **Cover** it.
4. **Write** it from memory, by hand if your exam is on paper and typed if it is on computer.
5. **Check** it, letter by letter. If it is wrong, go back to step 1.
6. **Use it** in one short sentence, because this helps it stay in your memory.

**Test the same words again** after 1 day, 3 days and 7 days. A word is finished only when you get it right all three times.

At 3 new words a day, Section 1 takes about 8 days and Section 4 takes about 3 weeks.

---

## 6. In the Exam

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| **While reading the question** | The question paper has the correct spelling of the topic words, so check them there whenever you use them. |
| **While writing** | If you are not sure of a word, use one you can spell: *convenient* → *easy*; *beneficial* → *helpful*. A simple correct word is better than a difficult wrong one. |
| **Last 3 minutes** | Read each paragraph **from the last sentence to the first**. You stop following the ideas and start seeing the words. |
| **Word endings** | Check -s, -ed, -ing, -ful and -ly. |

> On the computer-delivered test there is no spell check, so practise typing without it too. Turn it off when you type your practice essays.
