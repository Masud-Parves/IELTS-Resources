## Part 2 — Plan (5 min)

**Question**

> *Some people think that it is better for children to grow up in the countryside, while others believe that a city is a better place for them to grow up. Discuss both views and give your opinion.*

**One special rule for today: support the FIRST view (the countryside).** This practises the mistake from essay 2. My view must go **last**, so the order changes:

| Paragraph | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Intro** | City view first, then countryside. Then *I completely support **the latter** view because…* |
| **BP1** | The **city** view: *On the one hand, people who argue that…* (no "I") |
| **BP2** | The **countryside** view: *On the other hand, I firmly believe that…* |
| **Conclusion** | *While [city theme], I firmly believe that [countryside] because [the same reason].* |

**Write these before you start the essay:**

- One reason and one example for **the city** (BP1)
- One reason and one example for **the countryside** (BP2)
- My 3 opinion sentences (intro, BP2 topic sentence, conclusion), all with **the same reason**

## Part 4 — Check (5 min)

### Checklist A — Task Response

- ☐ My opinion sentence says **which view**: *I completely support **the latter** view because ___.*
- ☐ **My view goes last** in the intro (today: city first, countryside last).
- ☐ **One reason**, the same in the intro, the BP2 topic sentence and the conclusion.
- ☐ BP2 starts with ***I firmly believe***, not *critics argue* or *supporters believe*.
- ☐ No *also, both, equally, balance, not only… but also* in my opinion sentences.
- ☐ Examples start with ***a ___ who ___***, never *if any child*.
- ☐ The example shows **the sentence before it** (the 5-second test).
- ☐ BP2 has an **R sentence**: *Although some people argue that…, …*

### Checklist B — Grammar (read from the last sentence to the first)

| # | Check | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | After *should / will / can*: base form or passive | should not **gives** | should not **be given** |
| 2 | Subject–verb agreement | a child **live** | a child **lives** |
| 3 | Word forms | leads to **successful** · **equal** important | leads to **success** · **equally** important |
| 4 | Never start a sentence with *Which* | **Which** later led to… | **This** can later lead to… |
| 5 | No comma between two sentences | …city**, other** believe | …city**, while others** believe |
| 6 | Articles | grow up in countryside | grow up in **the** countryside |
| 7 | Fixed phrases | participate **on** · **less** or no · **in** time | participate **in** · **little** or no · **on** time |
| 8 | Uncountable nouns: no -s | schoolwork**s** · exercise**s** · leadership**s** | schoolwork · exercise · leadership |

### Checklist C — Mechanics

- ☐ A space after every full stop (*life. Therefore*)
- ☐ No contractions (*it's* → *it is*)
- ☐ No *he/she*: use *they* or plural nouns
- ☐ Spelling: **extracurricular**, **every day** (how often), **career**, **environment**
- ☐ Word count 260–299
